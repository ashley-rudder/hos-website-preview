§ 01Overview
A perception problem with the very people the brand needed most.

LACTAID had a perception problem with the very people it needed most. Millions of women in their mid-30s to late-40s live with mild dairy sensitivity and manage it quietly every day.

They do not call themselves lactose intolerant. They would not reach for a product that did. The brand needed to stop speaking the language of the medicine cabinet and start speaking the language of the table.

§ 02The Insight
The largest untapped audience was not looking for relief. They were looking for permission.

The category had spent decades optimizing for the symptomatic. The largest untapped audience wasn't looking for relief. They were looking for permission: to enjoy what they wanted, easily, without making an announcement of their limitations.

§ 03The Execution
An original musical brand platform built around a single irresistible hook.

Developed in 2025 under Ashley Rudder's tenure as Chief Creator Officer at Deutsch NY, "La La Lactaid" introduced an original musical brand platform built around a single, irresistible hook: "You can have your milk and smile."

The campaign ran across TV, CTV, and social, designed so the song functioned as both mnemonic and mood. The tone was joyful and aspirational by design.

This was the creative anchor of a 7-year Deutsch NY partnership with Lactaid, Kenvue, and HP Hood, and it marked a bold step forward in how the brand showed up in culture.
