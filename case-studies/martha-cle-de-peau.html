THE THIRST TRAP

She was already iconic. We just let the internet catch up.

Clé de Peau Beauté had never touched TikTok. The brief asked for a new generation of consumers. The real question was whether a brand synonymous with Japanese luxury could meet Gen Z on their terms without flinching. Martha Stewart had already gone viral on her own terms. We just let the internet catch up to what it already wanted.

Chapter 01
The System

Aesthetic. Archetype. Platform. Locked.

The system runs on three axes: porcelain CDP prestige restraint, the Aunty archetype voice, and TikTok-native ad logic embedded inside in-app features. Every piece built on this framework is invisible as advertising and impossible to dismiss as content.

Chapter 02
The Cluster

One Aunty. Five TikTok-native formats.

Every cut runs platform-native ad logic invisibly inside in-app features.
