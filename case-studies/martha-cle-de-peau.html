§ 01Overview
A prestige Japanese luxury brand had never touched TikTok. The stakes were precise.

Clé de Peau Beauté had never touched TikTok. The stakes were precise: enter the platform without diluting a prestige legacy built over decades. The brief asked for a new generation of consumers. The real question was whether a brand synonymous with Japanese luxury could meet Gen Z on their terms without flinching.

§ 02The Insight
The audience wasn't waiting to be introduced. They were already obsessed.

Martha Stewart had already gone viral on her own terms: a thirst trap selfie that stopped every feed it entered. "Gen Z loves a grey fox" was a top search term. The audience wasn't waiting to be introduced to her. They were already obsessed.

§ 03The Execution
A five-part TikTok-first series. Every piece felt discovered, not placed.

Ashley worked directly with Martha on strategy and copy to develop a five-part TikTok-first content series built entirely around platform-native formats: ASMR, beauty tutorials, clone trails, trending transitions.

Advertising best practices were embedded invisibly within in-app features, making every piece feel discovered rather than placed. Scripts were pressure-tested with Gen Z focus groups before production.

The "Aunty" archetype ran through every piece as the campaign's emotional spine: aspirational, irreverent, impossible to scroll past.
