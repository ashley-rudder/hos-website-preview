§ 01Overview
Most flavor campaigns announce. This one dramatized.

Most flavor campaigns announce. They list. They display. This one dramatized the moment of discovery.

Milk-sensitive women in their mid-30s to late-40s were already buying the brand. They just didn't know how far the flavor range went. Lactaid ice cream is made with 100% real milk and cream, just without the lactose. Nicole found that out on camera.

§ 02The Insight
The second reveal did more work than the first.

Discovery, when it hits the right person at the right moment, looks like mild disbelief quickly followed by something worse: finding out it's already gone.

The spot used two reveals in 15 seconds. The second one did more work than the first.

§ 03The Execution
A single comedic double-reveal grounded in a relatable domestic moment.

Developed in 2025 at Deutsch NY under Ashley Rudder as Chief Creator Officer, the 15-second TV spot centered on a single comedic double-reveal: Nicole just found out Lactaid ice cream is made with 100% real milk and cream, without the lactose. She also just found out her husband finished the last carton.

Humorous enough to cut through in 15 seconds. Grounded enough in a relatable domestic moment to feel earned.

The spot ran as part of the broader LACTAID platform relaunch alongside "La La Lactaid," expanding the brand's product conversation from milk to the full ice cream range.

§ 04The Results
